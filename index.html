Every relationship begins the same way and moves at the pace of your life — not a sales calendar. Nothing is implemented until you have seen the arithmetic behind it.

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Our story

    Independent since the first day

    Meridian & Vale was founded in 2003 by two advisers who wanted to give advice without a product shelf behind them. We have never sold a commissioned product, and we never will.

    What we believe

    That most financial outcomes are decided by a handful of choices — savings rate, asset allocation, tax location, and whether you stay invested. We spend our time on those four and treat everything else as noise.

    How we are paid

    One transparent fee: a flat planning engagement, or 0.65% of assets under management, tapering above $5M. No commissions, no revenue sharing, no third-party incentives of any kind.

    Who we serve

    Around 480 households — mid-career professionals, founders approaching an exit, and retired families protecting a legacy. We deliberately cap each adviser at 60 relationships.

    Trust & safeguards

    The protections behind the advice

    Independence is a structure, not a slogan. Here is how client assets are held, how we are supervised, and what you are entitled to see at any time.

    • Fiduciary in writing

      We are legally required to place your interests first, on every recommendation, and we put that obligation in the engagement letter.

    • Independent custody

      We never hold your money. Assets sit in your name at a third-party SIPC-member custodian; we hold trading authority only.

    • SOC 2 Type II

      Our systems complete an annual independent examination covering security, availability and confidentiality controls.

    • One disclosed fee

      Your quarterly statement shows the fee in dollars, not just basis points. No commissions, no revenue sharing, no soft dollars.

    • Open books

      Form ADV Parts 2A and 2B, our privacy notice and our conflicts register are available on request, before you sign anything.

    • Data discipline

      TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, mandatory MFA, and no sale or sharing of client data with third-party marketers. Ever.
